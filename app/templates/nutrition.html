Protein Distribution

Distribute 25-40g high-quality protein per meal across 3-4 daily feedings to sustain muscle protein synthesis (MPS) and stabilize blood glucose.

Hydration

Cellular Hydration

Aim for 30-40ml of water per kg of bodyweight daily. Replenish lost sodium, potassium, and magnesium after high-sweat sessions.

Recovery

Sleep & Tissue Repair

7 to 9 hours of quality sleep is when over 90% of growth hormone output and neurological adaptation occurs. Keep sleeping quarters cool and dark.
